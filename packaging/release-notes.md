## What's in Oley 2.1

- **17 lyric styles**: Paste-up, Polyester, ASCII, Editlyric (a 3D desk with a phone, laptop and typewriter), Glass, Warp, Voxel, Shadowplay, Foundry, Stack, Orbit, Constellation, Source, Nebula, Messages, Motion and Afterglow. Or **Auto**, which picks one that fits each song's mood.
- **ASCII now has 120 hand-built 3D scenes**: flowers, space, cars, cities, oceans and landscapes, picked by what each verse is about. The album cover shows briefly and never sits on the lyric.
- **Watch it your way**: full screen (Ctrl+Alt+L), in a floating window, or as your desktop wallpaper with Wallpaper Engine, plus a glow round your screen that pulses with the beat.
- **Make your own styles**: drop a `.js` file in the custom modes folder, or ask Claude to write one (see MAKING-MODES.md).

### Fixed in this release
- Orbit: one disc at the bottom; hover it and scroll to spin to the next or previous song like a revolver.
- Paste-up: the stop sign's pole no longer pokes through the sign.
- Editlyric: no more glare over the phone screen, and the papers no longer flicker into each other.
- Stack: no dots or dashes after words, and the highlight box never hides letters.
- Polyester, Voxel and Warp: titles and key words are never cut off or covered.
